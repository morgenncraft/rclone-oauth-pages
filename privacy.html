Privacy Policy

Rclone Drive Pardus is a personal OAuth application used only by the account owner.

The application does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

Google Drive data is accessed only by the account owner through rclone.

OAuth credentials and tokens remain on the owner's local device and are not transmitted to or stored by this website.

Access can be revoked at any time from the user's Google Account permissions.

This application is not affiliated with MorgenCraft or any business and does not serve third-party users.
